# OctWave 3.0

Official website for **OctWave 3.0**, Sri Lanka’s premier undergraduate AI and Machine Learning competition.

Organised by the **IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter** at the **University of Moratuwa**.

---

## About the competition

OctWave connects universities to industry through structured workshops, a Kaggle challenge, and a live final with real industry datasets. It is built for the next generation of AI engineers.

Open to undergraduate students from government and private institutions across Sri Lanka. Compete solo or as a team.

### Highlights

- 4 expert-led workshops
- Online Kaggle preliminary round
- Top 10 finalists
- Physical final at University of Moratuwa
- Industry-endorsed certificates, prizes, and network access

---

## Event structure

1. **Introductory session.** Industry experts set the foundation with AI/ML trends.
2. **Workshops (4 sessions).** Hands-on training in ML models, time series, data engineering, and Kaggle.
3. **Preliminary round (online).** Kaggle challenge and report submission. Top 10 teams advance.
4. **Final round (physical at UOM).** Real-world dataset, live presentations to an industry panel, and awards.

---

## Timeline

Five weeks from registration to the awards stage:

1. Register team *(registration closed)*
2. Attend workshops
3. Kaggle preliminary
4. Top-10 final at University of Moratuwa
5. Awards and recognition

---

## Who can take part

- Currently enrolled undergraduate students
- Teams of 1 to 4 members from the same university and academic batch
- Different faculties within the same institution are welcome

Full rules are available on the site.
